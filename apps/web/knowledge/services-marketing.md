# Raiwa Technologies — Marketing & Advertising Solutions

Raiwa Technologies plans and executes targeted digital marketing and advertising strategies to acquire customers and build long-term brand equity.

## Services
1. **Search Engine Optimization (SEO):**
   - On-page SEO: Keyword optimization, metadata, internal linking, content enhancement.
   - Technical SEO: Sitemap generation, robots.txt, schema markup, site speed optimization, mobile responsiveness.
   - Off-page SEO & Authority: High-quality backlinks, directory citations, digital PR.

2. **Social Media Marketing (SMM):**
   - Organic content calendar, social presence management (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, X).
   - High-engagement graphics, short-form reels/videos, and community management.

3. **Google Ads Management:**
   - Search Ads (high-intent keyword bidding).
   - Display & Discovery Ads for brand awareness.
   - Google Shopping ads for ecommerce merchants.
   - Performance Max (PMax) campaigns with smart bidding.
   - Retargeting and negative keyword pruning to eliminate wasted spend.

4. **Meta Ads Management (Facebook & Instagram):**
   - Audience targeting (Lookalikes, custom audiences, pixel retargeting).
   - Creative A/B testing: hooks, copy, formats (carousel, video, instant experience).
   - Conversion tracking using Meta Conversions API (CAPI).

5. **Marketing Automation & Email Marketing:**
   - Automated welcome sequences, abandoned cart recovery, retention newsletters.
