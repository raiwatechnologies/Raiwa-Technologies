# Raiwa Technologies — Ecommerce Solutions

Raiwa Technologies offers full-cycle marketplace store setup, optimization, advertising, and day-to-day management.

## Supported Marketplaces
- **Amazon:** Seller Central setup, brand registry, listing creation with high-converting copywriting, SEO keyword research, A+ content, Amazon PPC management, and account health compliance.
- **eBay:** Store setup, category mapping, optimized titles, template designs, customer support handling, feedback protection, and promotional tools.
- **Walmart Marketplace:** Seller application, catalog onboarding, two-day delivery badge setup, listing quality score improvement, and Walmart Sponsored Products ads.
- **Shopify:** Custom store design, theme setup, payment gateway integration, shipping rule configuration, app integrations, and conversion rate optimization (CRO).
- **TikTok Shop:** Social-commerce integration, product showcase setup, TikTok creator/affiliate outreach, viral video creative guidance, and flash deal coordination.
- **Daraz:** Store setup, product bundling, Daraz Mall qualification, campaign participation (11.11, 12.12), and seller rating protection.
- **Temu & Mercari:** Store onboarding, supplier coordination, listing management, and fast fulfillment alignment.

## Core Capabilities
1. **Catalog & Inventory Management:** Listing synchronization, stock monitoring, stock-out prevention.
2. **Account Health & Policy Protection:** Preventing suspension, handling customer metrics (ODR, VTR, LSR), resolving policy warnings.
3. **PPC & Advertising:** Marketplace-native ads to maximize return on ad spend (ROAS) and lower ACoS.
4. **Order & Return Processing:** Streamlined daily customer coordination and fulfillment management.
